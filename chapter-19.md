# Chapter 19 — Seo-jun's Problem

Seo-jun had made a mistake.
A serious one.
He had told Ha-Kim.
It had seemed like a good idea at the time.
Ha-Kim was his closest friend. He knew him better than almost anyone. If there was anyone Seo-jun could talk to about something like this, it was him.
Unfortunately, Seo-jun had forgotten one important detail.
Ha-Kim took everything seriously.
Very seriously.
───
"You're overthinking this."
Seo-jun pointed across the table.
"You have a laptop open."
Ha-Kim didn't look up.
"Yes."
"You're taking notes."
"Yes."
"Why?"
"I'm organizing my thoughts."
Seo-jun leaned forward.
"About my crush."
"Yes."
"Why?"
Ha-Kim finally looked at him.
"Because you asked me for advice."
"I asked whether I should ask her out."
"Exactly."
Seo-jun stared at him.
"That's not what normal people do."
Ha-Kim returned his attention to the screen.
"You came to the wrong person for normal."
Seo-jun sighed.
That was true.
───
Ha-eun sat on the couch beside them, quietly eating the snack she'd brought.
She watched Ha-Kim type.
Then looked at Seo-jun.
"How long has he been doing this?"
"Twenty minutes."
She blinked.
"Twenty?"
"Twenty-three."
Ha-eun slowly looked at Ha-Kim.
"You're counting?"
"I have a clock."
"That's not what I meant."
Ha-Kim ignored them.
Seo-jun leaned back.
"He's been asking questions for half an hour."
Ha-eun smiled.
"What kind of questions?"
Seo-jun began counting on his fingers.
"How long have I known her. How often I see her. What we talk about. What she likes. What I like about her."
Ha-eun's expression softened.
"That's actually a good question."
Seo-jun nodded.
"Right?"
Then Ha-Kim spoke.
"Also, why do you like her?"
Seo-jun froze.
He looked at him.
"That's not a question I can answer in one sentence."
Ha-Kim stopped typing.
"Why?"
Seo-jun thought about it.
For once, he didn't immediately make a joke.
"I don't know."
Ha-eun watched him.
Seo-jun looked down.
"I just..."
He rubbed the back of his neck.
"She's easy to be around."
Ha-Kim said nothing.
Seo-jun continued.
"She's quiet, but not boring. She actually listens when I talk. And she remembers stupid little things."
He smiled.
"She remembered my order before I even said it."
Ha-eun smiled too.
Seo-jun looked toward the window.
"And when I had a bad day, she noticed."
His voice became quieter.
"I didn't even tell her."
Ha-Kim closed the laptop.
Seo-jun looked at him.
"What?"
"You like her."
"I know."
"No."
Ha-Kim looked directly at him.
"You really like her."
Seo-jun was quiet.
Then he smiled.
"...Yeah."
───
The room became quiet for a moment.
Ha-eun looked between them.
"So..."
Seo-jun looked at her.
"What?"
"Ask her out."
Seo-jun blinked.
"Just like that?"
"Why not?"
He looked at Ha-Kim.
Ha-Kim nodded.
"She's right."
Seo-jun stared at both of them.
"That's it?"
Ha-Kim picked up his laptop again.
"Yes."
Seo-jun narrowed his eyes.
"Then why did you spend thirty minutes investigating everything?"
"I wanted to make sure."
"Make sure of what?"
"That you're not confusing loneliness with attraction."
Seo-jun stopped.
That question hit differently.
He leaned back.
For a moment, he thought about it.
Then shook his head.
"No."
Ha-Kim waited.
"I don't think I'm lonely."
Seo-jun smiled slightly.
"I have you."
Ha-Kim's expression didn't change.
Seo-jun continued.
"I have my studio. Friends. Work."
He looked down.
"I just want to see her."
Ha-Kim nodded once.
"Then ask her."
Seo-jun smiled.
"You're actually being helpful."
"I usually am."
"Don't ruin the moment."
───
The next afternoon, Seo-jun stood outside the café.
He had been there for almost five minutes.
He had already checked his reflection in the window twice.
He fixed his hair.
Then stopped.
"What am I doing?"
He took a breath.
"Just ask her."
He opened the door.
The bell rang.
Eun-bi looked up.
Her expression brightened.
"Seo-jun."
He smiled.
"Hey."
She noticed something.
"You look nervous."
"I'm not."
"You are."
He laughed awkwardly.
"Am I that obvious?"
"A little."
He walked to the counter.
She prepared his usual drink.
This time, however, Seo-jun didn't immediately take it.
"Eun-bi."
She looked up.
"Yes?"
"Are you busy after work?"
Her hands stopped.
"No."
"Would you..."
He hesitated.
Eun-bi waited.
"Would you want to get dinner with me?"
Her eyes widened slightly.
"Just dinner?"
Seo-jun smiled.
"If you want it to be just dinner."
She looked down.
A small smile appeared.
"I'd like that."
Seo-jun's shoulders relaxed.
"Really?"
She nodded.
"Really."
He grinned.
"Okay."
Eun-bi smiled back.
"Okay."
───
Seo-jun left the café a few minutes later.
He walked down the street.
Then stopped.
He looked at his phone.
Opened Ha-Kim's chat.
Typed:
I did it.
He stared at the message.
Then added:
She said yes.
He sent it.
Three seconds later, Ha-Kim replied.
Good.
Seo-jun frowned.
That's it?
He typed again.
That's all you have to say?
Ha-Kim replied:
What else should I say?
Seo-jun smiled.
I expected more.
The typing indicator appeared.
Then:
Don't make her regret saying yes.
Seo-jun's smile softened.
I won't.
He put his phone away.
Then immediately pulled it back out.
Also, stop researching her.
No response.
Seo-jun sighed.
"He's definitely still doing it."
───
That evening, Seo-jun arrived at Ha-Kim's apartment.
Ha-eun was already there.
She looked up from the couch.
"You did it?"
Seo-jun smiled proudly.
"She said yes."
Ha-eun's face brightened.
"Really?"
"Really."
"That's good."
Seo-jun sat down.
"Thank you."
Ha-eun smiled.
Then looked toward the kitchen.
"Ha-Kim?"
"He went to get water."
Seo-jun nodded.
A moment later, Ha-Kim walked back in.
Seo-jun pointed at him.
"Your investigation is over."
Ha-Kim sat down.
"Fine."
Seo-jun narrowed his eyes.
"Why did you say that so easily?"
"I got enough information."
"You researched her?"
"I looked at publicly available information."
"Ha-Kim."
"What?"
"You sound like a detective."
Ha-eun covered her mouth, trying not to laugh.
Ha-Kim looked at her.
"What?"
"Nothing."
Seo-jun leaned back.
"So what did you find?"
Ha-Kim paused.
"She's kind."
Seo-jun smiled.
"I already knew that."
"Hardworking."
"Also knew that."
"Quiet."
"Obviously."
"She seems to care about people."
Seo-jun's smile faded into something softer.
"Yeah."
Ha-Kim looked at him.
"That's what you like."
Seo-jun didn't answer immediately.
Then nodded.
"Yeah."
Ha-eun smiled.
"Then what's the problem?"
Seo-jun looked at her.
"I don't know."
He leaned back into the couch.
"I guess I'm scared I'll mess it up."
Ha-Kim looked at him.
"You probably will."
Seo-jun's eyes widened.
"WHAT?"
Ha-eun burst into a small laugh.
Ha-Kim continued calmly.
"Everyone makes mistakes."
Seo-jun stared at him.
"Could've phrased that better."
"But you won't know what works until you try."
Seo-jun became quiet.
Ha-Kim looked at him.
"Don't try to become someone she likes."
Seo-jun listened.
"Just be yourself."
Seo-jun smiled.
"That's surprisingly good advice."
"I know."
"You're still weird."
"I know."
Ha-eun smiled.
The three of them sat together in comfortable silence.
For once, Ha-Kim didn't open his laptop.
He didn't need to.
The investigation was over.
At least for now.
───
Later that night, Seo-jun walked home.
His phone buzzed.
A message from Eun-bi.
Thank you for today.
He smiled.
He replied:
I should be thanking you.
A few seconds later:
I'm looking forward to tomorrow.
Seo-jun stopped walking.
He stared at the message.
Then smiled so hard that someone passing by glanced at him.
He quickly looked away.
"Okay."
He put his phone in his pocket.
"Yeah."
He started walking again.
For the first time in a long time, tomorrow felt like something he was actually looking forward to.
And somewhere across Seoul, Eun-bi was probably smiling at her phone too.
Neither of them knew where this would go.
But for now...
They had a date.
