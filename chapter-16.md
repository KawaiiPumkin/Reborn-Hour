# Chapter 16 — The Difference

Saturday afternoon was quiet.

Ha-eun stood in front of Ha-Kim's apartment holding a small paper bag.

She knocked twice.

Nothing.

She knocked again.

Still nothing.

She looked at her phone.

No message.

Maybe he was sleeping.

After everything that happened with Min-seo, he had seemed a little distracted lately.

Ha-eun had noticed.

She just didn't know why.

She knocked one more time.

This time, she heard footsteps.

The door opened.

Ha-Kim stood there wearing a simple black shirt, his glasses slightly lower than usual.

He looked surprised.

"Ha-eun?"

She lifted the bag.

"I bought too much."

He looked inside.

"Again?"

"Yes."

"You always buy too much."

"It's not my fault."

"You bought two portions."

"Exactly."

He looked at her.

"That's not too much."

She smiled.

"Then let me in."

He stepped aside.

She walked in like she had done many times before.

She took off her shoes and placed the bag on the table.

"I got dumplings."

"Okay."

"And drinks."

"Okay."

"And—"

"You're staying."

She looked at him.

"Was I not allowed to?"

"I didn't say that."

She smiled.

"Good."

Ha-Kim went to the kitchen.

Ha-eun followed.

She opened the cabinet without asking and took out two plates.

Ha-Kim watched her.

"You know where everything is now."

"Obviously."

"That's concerning."

"You should be proud."

"I'm not."

She smiled.

He took the bag from her and started putting the food onto the plates.

Ha-eun sat down.

For a while, everything felt normal.

Then the doorbell rang.

Ha-Kim stopped.

Ha-eun looked at him.

"Are you expecting someone?"

"No."

He walked toward the door.

Ha-eun stayed at the table.

The bell rang again.

Ha-Kim was about to open it when his phone vibrated.

He looked at the screen.

His expression changed slightly.

Min-seo.

He had told her yesterday that he didn't want to meet yet.

Apparently, she hadn't listened.

The doorbell rang again.

Ha-eun stood up.

"I'll get it."

Before Ha-Kim could answer, she reached the door.

She opened it.

A woman stood outside.

Ha-eun froze.

The woman looked at her.

Then at the apartment.

Then back at her.

For a second, neither spoke.

"Uh..."

Ha-eun smiled awkwardly.

"Hi."

Min-seo blinked.

"Hi."

Ha-eun stepped aside slightly.

"Are you here for Ha-Kim?"

Min-seo looked past her.

"Yes."

"He's here."

She moved aside.

Min-seo entered.

Then she saw Ha-Kim standing a few steps away.

His expression was unreadable.

Min-seo looked at him.

"You didn't tell me you had company."

"I didn't know you were coming."

Her eyes moved toward Ha-eun.

Ha-eun gave a small polite smile.

"I'm Ha-eun."

Min-seo looked at her for another moment.

"Min-seo."

"Nice to meet you."

"Nice to meet you too."

There was no hostility.

Nothing dramatic.

Just an unfamiliar woman standing in Ha-Kim's apartment.

But Ha-eun noticed something.

Min-seo looked at Ha-Kim differently.

Not like a coworker.

Not like someone she had met recently.

Like someone she already knew.

Very well.

Ha-eun looked at Ha-Kim.

He noticed.

"She's someone I knew a long time ago."

That was all he said.

Ha-eun nodded.

"Oh."

She didn't ask anything else.

───

Min-seo sat on the couch.

Ha-eun returned to the table.

Ha-Kim stayed standing for a moment.

Then he looked at Ha-eun.

"You can stay."

She looked up.

"I know."

He walked over and sat beside her.

Min-seo watched them quietly.

Ha-eun opened the food container.

"I bought these."

Ha-Kim picked up a dumpling.

"You did."

"You sound surprised."

"I am."

"Why?"

"You usually complain about the price."

Ha-eun looked offended.

"I am allowed to care about money."

"I didn't say you weren't."

Min-seo smiled slightly.

"You two seem comfortable."

Ha-eun looked at her.

"We've gotten used to each other."

She didn't think much of the sentence.

But Min-seo did.

Ha-Kim noticed.

"How did you find me?"

Min-seo looked at him.

"Your number hasn't changed."

"I thought you deleted it."

"I tried."

Ha-Kim went quiet.

Ha-eun looked between them.

There was something there.

She just didn't know what.

Min-seo turned toward Ha-eun.

"How long have you known him?"

Ha-eun thought for a second.

"A few months."

"At work?"

"Yes."

Min-seo nodded.

"That makes sense."

Ha-eun tilted her head.

"Why?"

"You don't seem like someone Ha-Kim would normally let this close."

Ha-Kim looked at Min-seo.

"Min-seo."

She stopped.

There was no anger in his voice.

Just a warning.

She smiled.

"Sorry."

Ha-eun looked at him.

Then at her.

She didn't understand.

But she didn't ask.

───

A little later, Ha-eun got up to get drinks.

She opened the refrigerator.

"Which one do you want?"

Ha-Kim answered immediately.

"The left one."

She took it out.

Min-seo watched.

"You still drink that?"

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"Yes."

"You used to hate it."

"I changed my mind."

Min-seo smiled.

"You never changed your mind about anything."

Ha-Kim shrugged.

"I changed."

Ha-eun looked at him.

She couldn't help smiling.

He really had changed.

She had noticed that before.

But hearing someone from his past say it made her curious.

What had he been like before?

What had happened to him?

She wanted to ask.

But she didn't.

That was his life.

If he wanted her to know, he would tell her.

She brought the drinks over.

Then sat beside him again.

Their shoulders almost touched.

Ha-Kim didn't move away.

Neither did she.

Min-seo noticed.

But she said nothing.

───

After a while, Ha-eun checked the time.

"I should go."

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"Already?"

"I have some things to do."

He nodded.

"Okay."

Ha-eun stood.

She picked up her bag.

Min-seo watched her.

"It was nice meeting you."

Ha-eun smiled.

"You too."

She turned toward Ha-Kim.

"See you tomorrow."

"I'll walk you out."

"You don't have to."

"I know."

He stood anyway.

They walked to the door.

Ha-eun put on her shoes.

Then looked up at him.

"She knows you well."

Ha-Kim was quiet for a second.

"Yes."

Ha-eun nodded.

She didn't ask anything else.

Then she smiled.

"Goodbye."

"Goodbye."

He watched her leave.

The door closed.

Ha-Kim stayed there for a moment.

Min-seo was still sitting on the couch.

"You care about her."

Ha-Kim looked back.

"She's important to me."

Min-seo smiled faintly.

"You didn't say that about me."

"I didn't have to."

She looked at him.

"What does that mean?"

"It means you already knew."

Min-seo looked down.

She understood.

───

Outside, Ha-eun walked toward the elevator.

She pressed the button.

The doors opened.

She stepped inside.

As they closed, she leaned against the wall.

She wasn't upset.

Not really.

She just couldn't stop thinking about Min-seo.

The way she knew his habits.

The way she finished his sentences.

The way Ha-Kim had gone quiet around her.

There was a history there.

A big one.

Ha-eun didn't know what it was.

And she didn't think she had the right to ask.

Still...

She wondered what Ha-Kim had been like before she met him.

Maybe he had been happier.

Maybe he had someone important.

Maybe Min-seo was that person.

Ha-eun looked down at her hands.

She didn't like the thought.

That surprised her.

She had never thought of Ha-Kim as someone who could belong to somebody.

But suddenly, the possibility bothered her.

She shook her head.

"Stop."

The elevator reached her floor.

She stepped out.

She walked toward her apartment.

Then stopped.

She looked across the hall.

Ha-Kim's door was closed.

For some reason, she wanted to knock.

She didn't.

Instead, she went inside.

───

Back in Ha-Kim's apartment, Min-seo stood near the window.

"She's different."

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"Who?"

"Ha-eun."

He didn't answer.

"She's comfortable around you."

"Yes."

"You let her be."

"Yes."

Min-seo turned toward him.

"You never used to do that."

Ha-Kim looked down.

"I was different."

"You really were."

She walked closer.

"I want to know who you became."

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"Why?"

"Because I still care about you."

He didn't look away.

"I know."

"And I want another chance."

Silence.

Ha-Kim thought about the past.

About being seventeen.

About the person he had been before everything changed.

About the girl he had loved at that age.

Min-seo had been part of a life that felt impossibly far away now.

He still cared.

He couldn't lie about that.

But caring about someone wasn't the same as wanting the past back.

He looked toward the door.

Ha-eun had left only a few minutes ago.

He remembered the way she had stood in his kitchen.

The way she knew where everything was.

The way she had bought food because she thought he might want it.

The way she had smiled when he said her cooking was good.

The way she had looked at him before leaving.

Something about her presence felt easy.

Natural.

He didn't have to become the person he used to be around her.

He could simply be himself.

Min-seo watched his expression.

"You really are different."

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"So are you."

She smiled.

"Maybe."

───

The next morning, Ha-eun arrived at work.

She saw Ha-Kim almost immediately.

He looked toward her.

Their eyes met.

Usually, she would walk over.

Today, she hesitated.

Then she smiled.

"Morning."

"Morning."

She sat at her desk.

Ha-Kim watched her for a moment.

Something was off.

Not much.

Just enough.

He walked over.

"Are you okay?"

"Yeah."

"You sure?"

She nodded.

"I'm fine."

He studied her face.

She smiled again.

This time, it looked more forced.

But he didn't push.

"Okay."

He returned to his desk.

Ha-eun watched him go.

She wanted to ask him about Min-seo.

She wanted to know who she was.

Why she knew him so well.

Why she had come to his apartment.

But she couldn't bring herself to ask.

Not yet.

And Ha-Kim...

He was beginning to understand something too.

The person he remembered from years ago was still somewhere inside his memories.

He still cared about her.

But the feeling belonged to a different time.

A different version of himself.

The person standing beside him now was not the girl from his memories.

She was someone new.

And the woman who had walked into his life recently...

Was someone new too.

Ha-Kim looked across the office.

Ha-eun was quietly working at her desk.

She looked up.

Caught him staring.

Her eyes widened.

He looked away.

She smiled.

And for some reason, that small smile stayed in his mind.

The past had returned.

But Ha-Kim was beginning to understand that returning to the past...

Didn't mean he belonged there anymore.
