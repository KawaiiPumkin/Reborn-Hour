# You're Training Her

The next morning, Ha-eun arrived at Haetae Cyber earlier than usual.

She stood outside the enormous building and took a deep breath.

"Okay."

She looked at the entrance.

"Don't embarrass yourself today."

She walked inside.

The building was already busy.

Employees were walking around with coffee in their hands. Some were talking about work while others were staring at their computers.

Ha-eun looked around.

Everything still felt unfamiliar.

She spotted the manager and quickly walked toward him.

"Good morning."

The manager looked at her.

"You're early."

"Yeah."

He looked at his watch.

"Your trainer should already be here."

Ha-eun nodded.

"Okay."

She looked around the office.

Then she saw him.

Ha-Kim was already sitting at his desk.

Same black clothes.

Same glasses.

Same messy hair.

Same expression that made him look like he hadn't slept in years.

Ha-eun slowly walked toward him.

"Good morning."

Ha-Kim looked up.

"Morning."

She sat in the chair beside him.

Ha-Kim looked at her computer.

"Turn it on."

"Okay."

She turned it on.

He waited.

Ha-eun looked at him.

"Is that it?"

"For now."

"Oh."

Ha-Kim opened the training program.

"Yesterday we covered the basics. Today we're going to do something slightly harder."

Ha-eun became nervous.

"How much harder?"

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"Not very."

She relaxed.

"Okay."

"Unless you make it harder."

Her eyes widened.

"What does that mean?"

"You have a talent for clicking things you shouldn't."

Ha-eun stared at him.

"I only did that once."

"Yesterday."

"Still."

"Once was enough."

She puffed out her cheeks.

"You're annoying."

Ha-Kim looked back at his screen.

"I've been told."

Ha-eun tried not to smile.

She failed.

Ha-Kim noticed but didn't say anything.

He started explaining the task.

"This is a basic security check. You need to find the problem in the system."

Ha-eun stared at the screen.

"How?"

"Look for anything unusual."

She stared harder.

"That's it?"

"That's it."

She looked at the screen again.

There were lines of information everywhere.

Her eyes slowly widened.

"I don't see anything."

"Keep looking."

She kept looking.

Five minutes passed.

Nothing.

Ten minutes.

Still nothing.

Ha-eun leaned closer to the screen.

"Is this a trick?"

"No."

"Are you sure?"

"Yes."

She looked at him.

"Are you making fun of me?"

Ha-Kim turned toward her.

"No."

"You look like you're making fun of me."

"I'm not."

"You always look like that."

Ha-Kim paused.

"Like what?"

"Like you're judging everyone."

"I don't judge everyone."

Ha-eun raised an eyebrow.

"You definitely look like you do."

Ha-Kim turned back to his computer.

"I'll take that as feedback."

Ha-eun smiled.

She went back to the task.

A few minutes later, she clicked something.

The screen changed.

She froze.

"...I did it again."

Ha-Kim looked at the screen.

"You clicked the wrong option."

"Sorry."

"Don't apologize."

She looked at him.

"Right."

He pointed at the screen.

"Undo it."

She followed his instructions.

"Like this?"

"Yes."

She clicked.

The screen returned to normal.

Ha-eun smiled.

"Okay."

Ha-Kim nodded.

"Good."

She looked at him.

"Was that actually good?"

"Yes."

She smiled.

"Thanks."

He turned back toward his screen.

Ha-eun continued working.

This time, she paid more attention.

She checked every line carefully.

She didn't rush.

After a while, she noticed something.

"Wait."

Ha-Kim looked over.

"I think I found something."

He looked at her screen.

"Where?"

She pointed.

"Here."

Ha-Kim looked closely.

There was a small mistake in the system.

He nodded.

"Good."

Ha-eun's eyes widened.

"Really?"

"Yes."

She smiled.

"I found it."

"You did."

She looked proud of herself.

Ha-Kim looked back at his computer.

He didn't say anything else.

But inside, he was surprised.

She actually noticed it.

She wasn't good yet.

Not even close.

But she was learning.

And more importantly, she was paying attention.

That mattered.

───

Around noon, Ha-eun was still working.

Her stomach suddenly made a loud noise.

She froze.

Ha-Kim slowly looked at her.

"...You didn't hear that."

"I did."

"No, you didn't."

"I did."

She covered her stomach with her hands.

"I forgot to eat breakfast."

Ha-Kim looked at the clock.

"You should eat."

"I don't have anything."

He looked at her.

"You didn't bring food?"

She shook her head.

"I didn't have time."

Ha-Kim stared at her for a moment.

Then he stood up.

"Where are you going?"

"Coffee."

"Oh."

He walked away.

Ha-eun watched him leave.

A few minutes later, he came back with two drinks.

He placed one beside her.

She looked at it.

"What's this?"

"Coffee."

"I can see that."

"Then why ask?"

Ha-eun stared at him.

"You really are annoying."

Ha-Kim sat down.

"Drink it."

She picked it up.

"Thanks."

He nodded.

Ha-eun took a sip.

Her eyes widened.

"It's good."

Ha-Kim continued working.

"You like coffee?"

"Yeah."

"Then drink it."

She smiled.

For a moment, neither of them spoke.

Ha-eun looked around the office.

Everyone seemed busy.

She noticed Ha-Kim barely interacted with anyone.

"Do you always sit alone?"

"Mostly."

"Don't you have friends here?"

"No."

She looked surprised.

"None?"

"No."

"Why?"

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"Because I don't talk to people."

Ha-eun laughed quietly.

"At least you're honest."

He looked back at his computer.

"You talk too much."

"What?"

"Compared to me."

She smiled.

"That's not difficult."

Ha-Kim almost smiled.

Almost.

───

After lunch, Ha-eun returned to training.

This time, Ha-Kim gave her a more difficult task.

She stared at the screen.

"I don't know how to do this."

"Try."

"I might mess it up."

"You will."

Ha-eun looked at him.

"Excuse me?"

"You've made mistakes every day so far."

"That's not encouraging."

"It's not supposed to be."

She sighed.

"Fine."

She started working.

The first attempt failed.

The second attempt failed.

The third attempt failed too.

Ha-eun dropped her hands onto the desk.

"I can't do this."

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"You can."

"No, I can't."

"You're just frustrated."

"I've been trying for an hour."

"Then try for another hour."

She stared at him.

"You're seriously terrible at encouraging people."

"I wasn't trying to encourage you."

"Then what are you trying to do?"

"Teach you."

Ha-eun went quiet.

Ha-Kim pointed toward the screen.

"Look at your first attempt."

She looked.

"You made this mistake."

He pointed at another part.

"Then you fixed it here."

He pointed again.

"But you made another mistake here."

Ha-eun looked closely.

"So..."

"You are improving."

She blinked.

"Really?"

"Yes."

He turned back to his computer.

"You just don't notice because you're looking at the mistakes."

Ha-eun stared at him.

For some reason, those words stayed in her mind.

She looked back at the screen.

Then she started again.

This time, she didn't rush.

She carefully followed each step.

A few minutes later, she stopped.

"I think..."

She clicked.

The system responded correctly.

Ha-eun stared at the screen.

"I did it."

Ha-Kim looked over.

He checked her work.

"Correct."

She smiled.

"Yes!"

A few employees nearby looked over.

Ha-eun quickly lowered her voice.

"Sorry."

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"For what?"

"I got excited."

"That's fine."

She smiled again.

"Thanks."

───

Later that afternoon, the manager walked past their desks.

He stopped.

"How is she doing?"

Ha-Kim looked at Ha-eun's screen.

"She's improving."

The manager raised an eyebrow.

"Already?"

Ha-eun looked offended.

"What do you mean, already?"

The manager smiled.

"I didn't say anything."

Ha-Kim returned to his work.

The manager looked at Ha-eun.

"Keep working hard."

"I will."

He walked away.

Ha-eun looked at Ha-Kim.

"Does he always talk like that?"

"Yes."

"He's scary."

"He knows."

She laughed.

Ha-Kim continued working.

A few seconds later, the manager came back.

"Ha-Kim."

He looked up.

"Yes?"

"Keep training her."

Ha-Kim sighed.

"I am."

"Good."

The manager looked at Ha-eun.

"She still has a lot to learn."

Ha-eun lowered her head.

The manager left.

Ha-Kim noticed.

"Don't worry about it."

She looked at him.

"I feel like I'm slowing you down."

"You are."

Her expression dropped.

Ha-Kim continued.

"But that's what training is for."

Ha-eun looked at him.

"Oh."

He looked back at his screen.

"You'll get better."

She smiled.

"Okay."

───

Near the end of the workday, Ha-eun stood up to get some water.

As she walked toward the break area, she saw a familiar face.

It was the man from her interview.

The same tall man who had given her number 47.

She stopped.

"Excuse me."

He looked at her.

"Yes?"

"You were at my interview."

He nodded.

"I remember you."

Ha-eun smiled awkwardly.

"You're the one who gave me my number."

"That's right."

She looked at him.

"What's your name?"

He paused.

"Han Ji-hoon."

"Oh."

She nodded.

"I'm Choi Ha-eun."

"I know."

She froze.

"How does everyone here already know my name?"

Ji-hoon looked at her for a moment.

"You're the new employee."

"Oh."

That made sense.

Ji-hoon glanced toward Ha-Kim's desk.

"You're being trained by him?"

"Yeah."

Ji-hoon looked back at her.

"Good luck."

Ha-eun looked confused.

"Why?"

Ji-hoon smiled slightly.

"You're going to need it."

He walked away.

Ha-eun stared after him.

"What was that supposed to mean?"

She looked toward Ha-Kim.

He was still sitting at his desk.

She walked back.

"Your coworker is strange."

Ha-Kim looked up.

"Which one?"

"The tall guy."

"Han Ji-hoon?"

"Yeah."

Ha-Kim paused for half a second.

"He's fine."

Ha-eun sat down.

"He told me good luck because you're training me."

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"He's not wrong."

She stared at him.

"You agree?"

"Yes."

Ha-eun crossed her arms.

"You're both annoying."

Ha-Kim looked at her.

For a moment, the corner of his mouth almost moved.

Ha-eun noticed.

"Did you just smile?"

"No."

"You did."

"No."

"You definitely did."

Ha-Kim turned back toward his computer.

"Go home."

Ha-eun laughed.

"Fine."

She packed her things.

Before leaving, she looked at him.

"See you tomorrow."

Ha-Kim nodded.

"Tomorrow."

She walked away.

Ha-Kim watched her for a few seconds before returning to his screen.

He looked at the training report.

Her progress was still slow.

She made too many mistakes.

She had almost no experience.

But she kept trying.

Most people gave up when they failed enough times.

Ha-eun didn't.

That was what caught his attention.

He closed the report.

"She's not hopeless."

He looked toward the office entrance.

Ha-eun was already gone.

Ha-Kim sighed.

"Still annoying, though."

And for the first time, he wasn't completely sure he meant it.
