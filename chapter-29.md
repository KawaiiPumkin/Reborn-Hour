Chapter 29 — One Hour
00:59:46
The blade rose.
Ha-Kim's blood went cold.
He knew what he was seeing.
The payment.
The address.
The promise that everything would end.
It had all been a lie.
Ha-eun had walked straight into a trap.
The man stepped toward her.
Ha-Kim tried to move.
He couldn't.
He was still inside the vision.
All he could do was watch.
00:59:32
Ha-eun backed away.
Her eyes moved around the street.
Three men.
She hadn't expected three.
One stood behind her.
Another blocked the street.
The man holding the blade stood in front.
Ha-eun's hand slowly moved toward her phone.
The man noticed.
"Don't."
She stopped.
Ha-Kim's jaw tightened.
He knew what she was thinking.
She wanted to call him.
She should have.
But she hadn't.
And now—
The man stepped closer.
"You should've brought more."
"I brought everything I had."
"Not enough."
"I told you I don't have anything else."
He grabbed her arm.
Ha-eun flinched.
Ha-Kim's hands curled into fists.
He couldn't hear himself.
Couldn't reach her.
But he understood enough.
This wasn't about money.
They wanted her gone.
00:58:41
Ha-eun struggled against his grip.
"Let go of me."
The man pulled her closer.
"You really thought we'd let you walk away?"
Ha-eun's face changed.
She finally understood.
Ha-Kim saw it too.
The moment she realized there was no deal.
No last payment.
No freedom.
She had been brought there alone because they wanted her alone.
The man raised the blade again.
Ha-Kim's eyes widened.
The vision shook.
His head suddenly throbbed.
He saw rain.
Ha-eun.
The blade.
Then darkness.
The image disappeared.
Ha-Kim gasped.
───
He slammed back into reality.
His hand was still gripping the kitchen counter.
The apartment came back into focus.
Rain against the windows.
The refrigerator humming.
The food he had been preparing.
His phone on the floor.
Ha-Kim stood completely still.
His breathing was heavy.
For a moment, he couldn't process what he had seen.
Then—
"Ha-eun."
He grabbed his phone.
Called her.
Ring.
Ring.
Nothing.
Again.
Nothing.
His heart started pounding.
He checked the time.
The vision had shown him the countdown.
He remembered the address.
He remembered the street.
He remembered the men.
He knew exactly where she was.
Ha-Kim grabbed his jacket.
Keys.
Wallet.
Phone.
He didn't even turn off the stove.
He ran out.
───
The rain hit him the second he stepped outside.
He didn't stop.
He ran down the street.
His breathing was already becoming uneven.
His head hurt.
His vision blurred slightly.
He ignored it.
Ha-eun was there.
Somewhere ahead.
And every second mattered.
He called her again.
Nothing.
"Pick up."
Again.
Nothing.
Ha-Kim shoved the phone into his pocket and kept running.
He knew the route now.
The vision had shown him enough.
Left at the intersection.
Straight past the convenience store.
Then the older buildings.
He ran faster.
Cars passed beside him.
Water splashed against his shoes.
His glasses became covered with rain.
He wiped them with his sleeve without slowing down.
His head throbbed harder.
He nearly stumbled.
He caught himself.
Kept going.
Not now.
He couldn't stop now.
───
The streets became quieter.
The buildings older.
Ha-Kim recognized the area.
His heart dropped.
This was it.
The same narrow road from the vision.
He looked ahead.
Nothing.
No Ha-eun.
No men.
Just rain.
For one terrifying second, he wondered if he had remembered wrong.
Then he heard something.
A voice.
Faint.
Coming from somewhere ahead.
Ha-Kim stopped.
His breathing slowed.
He listened.
Nothing.
Then—
"Please..."
Ha-eun.
His eyes widened.
He ran toward the sound.
───
The building stood at the end of the street.
An old metal door.
Ha-Kim reached it.
His hand touched the handle.
He could hear voices inside.
One of them was hers.
"Let me go."
Another voice answered.
"You should've thought about that before you ran."
Ha-Kim's expression changed.
His hand tightened around the handle.
He didn't know what was happening inside.
He didn't know how many people were there.
He didn't know what would happen if he went in.
But he knew one thing.
He was not too late.
Not yet.
Ha-Kim pushed the door open.
───
Inside, Ha-eun looked up.
Her eyes widened.
"Ha-Kim...?"
For a second, neither of them moved.
Then Ha-Kim stepped inside.
His eyes went straight to the man standing beside her.
And the blade in his hand.
His expression went cold.
"Get away from her."
The man turned.
Ha-eun stared at Ha-Kim in disbelief.
"You came..."
Ha-Kim didn't look away from the attacker.
"Of course I did."
The man raised the blade.
Ha-Kim stepped forward.
And the moment he had been trying to prevent—
was finally beginning.
