# Chapter 21 — I'll Find You
By Monday morning, Ha-eun had discovered something strange.
Kang Seo-jun was smiling too much.
Not his normal smile.
Seo-jun always smiled.
He smiled when talking.
He smiled when annoying Ha-Kim.
He sometimes smiled before annoying Ha-Kim, which was usually how Ha-Kim knew something terrible was about to happen.
But this was different.
Ha-eun slowly leaned toward Ha-Kim.
"Look at him."
Ha-Kim continued typing.
"No."
"Just look."
"No."
"Why?"
"I know what he looks like."
Ha-eun frowned.
"Something is wrong with him."
"Many things."
"Ha-Kim."
He finally looked.
Seo-jun was standing several desks away, staring at his phone.
Smiling.
Then typing.
Then smiling again.
Ha-Kim looked back at his screen.
"He's texting Eun-bi."
Ha-eun stared at him.
"How do you know?"
"He told me."
"When?"
"Saturday."
"He told you before me?"
"Apparently."
Ha-eun looked offended.
"Wow."
Ha-Kim glanced at her.
"What?"
"I thought we were friends."
"You are."
"Then why do you get information first?"
"I've known him longer."
"That's unfair."
"That's how time works."
Ha-eun opened her mouth.
Closed it.
Then pointed at him.
"I don't like that answer."
"I know."
Across the office, Seo-jun suddenly looked up.
He noticed both of them staring at him.
"What?"
Ha-eun immediately smiled.
"Nothing."
Seo-jun narrowed his eyes.
"Why are you looking at me like that?"
"We aren't."
"You are."
Ha-Kim returned to work.
Ha-eun didn't.
"So."
Seo-jun already knew.
"No."
"How was your date?"
Several nearby employees looked over.
Seo-jun's face changed immediately.
"Why are you so loud?"
Ha-eun's eyes widened.
"I wasn't loud."
"You absolutely were."
Ji-woo appeared from behind her monitor.
"Date?"
Seo-jun froze.
Ha-eun covered her mouth.
"Oh."
Ji-woo looked between them.
"What date?"
"Nothing."
"With who?"
"Nobody."
Ha-Kim continued typing.
"Eun-bi."
Seo-jun slowly turned toward him.
"You."
Ha-Kim looked up.
"What?"
"Traitor."
"You didn't tell me it was confidential."
"It was implied."
"No."
Ji-woo leaned forward.
"Who's Eun-bi?"
Ha-eun was already smiling again.
"The café girl."
Seo-jun pointed at her.
"You're also a traitor."
"I barely know you."
"That's worse."
Ji-woo looked delighted.
"Wait. The café downstairs?"
"Nearby," Ha-eun corrected.
"The cute barista?"
Seo-jun immediately looked at her.
"You think she's cute?"
Everyone became quiet.
Seo-jun realized what he'd said.
Ha-eun slowly turned toward Ha-Kim.
Ha-Kim looked back.
Neither said anything.
Seo-jun rubbed his face.
"Forget that."
Ji-woo grinned.
"Oh, you like her."
"I have work."
"You definitely like her."
"I suddenly have a lot of work."
He walked away.
Ha-eun watched him go.
Then whispered:
"He definitely likes her."
Ha-Kim nodded.
"Obviously."
───
At lunch, Seo-jun disappeared.
Ha-eun knew exactly where he went.
Twenty minutes later, he returned carrying coffee.
One cup.
Ha-eun stared at it.
Seo-jun stopped.
"What?"
"Nothing."
"Stop doing that."
"Doing what?"
"Looking at me like you know something."
Ha-eun smiled innocently.
"I don't know anything."
Ha-Kim looked at the cup.
"She wrote something on it."
Seo-jun immediately covered the side with his hand.
Ha-eun nearly fell out of her chair.
"SHE WROTE SOMETHING?"
Ha-Kim's mouth moved slightly.
Seo-jun looked betrayed.
"How did you even see that?"
"Eyes."
"Can both of you leave me alone?"
"No," Ha-eun said.
"Yes," Ha-Kim said at the same time.
Seo-jun looked at Ha-Kim.
"Thank you."
Ha-Kim returned to his monitor.
Ha-eun looked at him.
"You're boring."
"I know."
She leaned closer.
"What did she write?"
Ha-Kim glanced at Seo-jun.
Seo-jun was already walking away.
"I couldn't read it."
Ha-eun sighed.
"Useless."
Ha-Kim looked at her.
"You've become very comfortable insulting me."
She smiled.
"I know."
He almost smiled too.
───
The afternoon was quieter.
Ha-eun finished a report without asking Ha-Kim for help.
That alone would've been impossible a few months ago.
She checked it twice.
Then once more because she didn't trust herself.
Finally, she sent it.
Ha-Kim noticed.
"You checked that three times."
Ha-eun looked over.
"How do you know?"
"I saw you."
"Stop watching me."
"You sit beside me."
"Still creepy."
"You made one mistake."
Her expression collapsed.
"Where?"
Ha-Kim pointed at her monitor.
She immediately leaned forward.
Then stopped.
There wasn't one.
Ha-eun slowly looked at him.
Ha-Kim's face remained completely serious.
"You lied."
"Yes."
She stared at him.
"You made me panic."
"Yes."
"Why?"
He shrugged.
Ha-eun grabbed a sticky note.
Ha-Kim saw it coming.
"Don't."
She threw it anyway.
It landed on his shoulder.
He looked at it.
Then at her.
Ha-eun smiled proudly.
"Good."
"Very mature."
"Thank you."
He removed the sticky note.
Then stuck it to her forehead.
Ha-eun froze.
Ji-woo saw it from across the desk.
She burst out laughing.
Ha-eun pulled the note off.
"HA-KIM."
He was already typing again.
"What?"
She stared at him.
Then started laughing too.
For a moment, the office felt completely ordinary.
───
At 4:32 PM, the elevator doors opened.
Ha-Kim didn't notice.
Ha-eun did.
Her smile faded slightly.
A woman stepped into the office.
Long dark-brown hair.
Glasses.
Confident posture.
Min-seo.
Ha-eun hadn't seen her since everything changed between her and Ha-Kim.
Min-seo looked around.
Then found him.
She walked toward his desk.
Ha-Kim noticed her footsteps.
He looked up.
His expression immediately became quieter.
"Min-seo."
"We need to talk."
Ha-eun looked down at her monitor.
She tried to focus.
She wasn't going to listen.
She was his girlfriend now.
She trusted him.
Still...
Her ears unfortunately worked.
Ha-Kim stood.
"Outside."
Min-seo nodded.
They walked toward the elevators.
Ha-eun watched them for half a second.
Then forced herself back toward her screen.
Ji-woo looked at her.
Ha-eun immediately said:
"Don't."
Ji-woo raised both hands.
"I didn't say anything."
"You were going to."
"Maybe."
Ha-eun sighed.
"I'm fine."
Ji-woo studied her.
"Okay."
And surprisingly, she left it there.
───
Ha-Kim and Min-seo stopped near an empty hallway.
She crossed her arms.
"You blocked me."
Ha-Kim looked at her.
"Yes."
Min-seo stared at him.
"You could at least pretend to feel bad."
"I didn't want to lie."
Her jaw tightened.
"Why?"
"You kept calling."
"Because you stopped answering."
"That's usually what not answering means."
"Ha-Kim."
He became quiet.
Min-seo looked away.
For a moment, the confidence disappeared.
"You really don't want me in your life anymore?"
Ha-Kim didn't answer immediately.
"It's not that simple."
"Then explain it."
He looked toward the windows.
Seoul stretched far below them.
"I don't hate you."
"I know."
"I don't regret everything either."
Min-seo looked at him.
"But?"
"But that doesn't mean we should go back."
She was quiet.
Ha-Kim continued.
"We were different people."
"People change."
"Exactly."
That hurt more than she expected.
Min-seo looked down.
"Is it because of her?"
Ha-Kim knew exactly who she meant.
"No."
Min-seo looked back up.
His answer surprised her.
"No?"
"I'm with Ha-eun because I want to be with Ha-eun."
He paused.
"But I'm not with you because what we had ended a long time ago."
Min-seo said nothing.
"I'm not choosing between you."
That landed harder.
There wasn't anger in his voice.
No cruelty.
Just certainty.
Min-seo looked through the glass wall toward the office.
Ha-eun was sitting at her desk.
She was pretending not to look.
Badly.
Min-seo almost smiled.
"She's terrible at pretending."
Ha-Kim glanced back.
Ha-eun immediately looked at her monitor.
"Yes."
Min-seo looked at him.
"You noticed."
"Obviously."
Something changed in Min-seo's expression.
Small.
Almost sad.
"You really like her."
Ha-Kim didn't hesitate.
"Yes."
Min-seo swallowed.
Then nodded.
"Okay."
Ha-Kim looked at her.
"Okay?"
"I didn't say I like it."
"I didn't expect you to."
"I also didn't say I'm happy about it."
"Okay."
She frowned.
"You're still annoying."
"So I've heard."
For the first time, Min-seo smiled slightly.
Not because she was happy.
Maybe because, for one second, he sounded like someone she remembered.
Then the smile disappeared.
"I'll stop calling."
Ha-Kim nodded.
"Thank you."
She started walking away.
Then stopped.
"Ha-Kim."
He looked at her.
"I really did love you."
His expression softened.
"I know."
Min-seo nodded once.
Then left.
───
Ha-Kim returned to his desk.
Ha-eun stared very intensely at her computer.
Too intensely.
He sat down.
Nothing happened.
Thirty seconds.
One minute.
Two.
Finally:
"You're not going to ask?"
Ha-eun kept looking forward.
"No."
"Okay."
Another ten seconds.
"What did she want?"
Ha-Kim looked at her.
Ha-eun's face turned slightly pink.
"You said you weren't going to ask."
"I changed my mind."
"She was upset because I blocked her."
Ha-eun blinked.
"You blocked her?"
"Yes."
"Why?"
"She kept contacting me."
Ha-eun tried very hard not to look pleased.
She failed.
Ha-Kim noticed.
"You're smiling."
"I'm not."
"You are."
"No."
"Ha-eun."
She covered her mouth.
"I'm sorry."
"You're not."
"No."
Ha-Kim looked back at his screen.
Ha-eun slowly leaned toward him.
"Did you tell her about us?"
"She already knew."
"Oh."
A small silence.
Then Ha-eun quietly asked:
"And?"
Ha-Kim looked at her.
"I told her I want to be with you."
Ha-eun froze.
Her face immediately became red.
Ha-Kim returned to his work.
She stared at him.
"You can't just say things like that."
"I just did."
"At work."
"You asked."
"Still."
He glanced at her.
Ha-eun looked down.
A tiny smile stayed on her face for the rest of the afternoon.
───
Across Seoul, someone opened an old file.
The room was dark except for the computer screen.
There wasn't much inside.
A name.
An age.
Old records.
Several addresses that were no longer useful.
The person scrolled slowly.
HA-KIM
Twenty-four.
Most of the information ended years ago.
School records.
An old photograph.
Then almost nothing.
As if he had simply disappeared.
The person leaned closer.
A second monitor displayed information about Haetae Cyber.
Employee directories.
Public company records.
News articles.
None of it seemed important.
The cursor moved past them.
The company wasn't the target.
The CEO wasn't the target.
One name was.
Ha-Kim.
A phone vibrated beside the keyboard.
The person answered.
"Anything?"
A voice spoke from the other end.
"No confirmed address."
"What about the company?"
"Possibly."
The person stared at the screen.
"Possibly isn't enough."
"We're checking."
Silence.
Then:
"He stayed hidden longer than I expected."
The person opened the old photograph.
A much younger Ha-Kim stared back.
No glasses.
Different hair.
Different face.
But unmistakably him.
The stranger touched the edge of the screen.
"Keep looking."
The call ended.
Another file opened.
This one contained a location.
Seoul.
Recent.
The stranger leaned back.
For the first time, they smiled.
"I'll find you."
───
Thunder returned that night.
Not as violently as before.
Just enough to shake the windows occasionally.
Ha-Kim was in the kitchen when someone knocked.
He already knew who it was.
He opened the door.
Ha-eun stood there.
She was holding a pillow.
Ha-Kim stared at it.
Then at her.
"What?"
"I'm sleeping here."
He looked across the hall.
Her apartment door was open.
"Why?"
Ha-eun walked past him.
"Your bed is comfortable."
Ha-Kim closed the door.
"You have a bed."
"Yours is more comfortable."
"They're the same type of mattress."
Ha-eun stopped.
She slowly turned around.
"...I like your pillows."
"You complained about them last week."
Her eyes narrowed.
"Ha-Kim."
"What?"
"Stop ruining my excuses."
He stared at her.
She hugged the pillow against herself.
A tiny smile appeared on his face.
"You don't need an excuse."
Ha-eun's expression changed.
Just slightly.
Then she looked away.
"I know."
She walked toward his bedroom.
Ha-Kim watched her.
"Are you at least going to use your own pillow?"
"No."
"Then why did you bring it?"
Ha-eun stopped.
She looked at the pillow in her hands.
Then back at him.
"I don't know."
Ha-Kim laughed.
"Shut up."
"I didn't say anything."
"You're laughing."
"A little."
She threw the pillow at him.
He caught it.
───
Later, Ha-eun lay beside him.
The room was dark.
Rain tapped against the window.
She moved closer until her forehead rested against his chest.
Ha-Kim looked down.
"Comfortable bed?"
"Very."
"The bed specifically?"
"Yes."
"Nothing else?"
Ha-eun pinched his side.
He flinched.
"Ow."
"Sleep."
Ha-Kim smiled.
A few seconds passed.
Ha-eun quietly reached for his hand beneath the blanket.
Their fingers intertwined.
"Ha-Kim?"
"Yeah?"
"I'm probably sleeping here tomorrow too."
He looked at her.
"What happened to your apartment?"
"Nothing."
"Then why?"
She closed her eyes.
"Your bed is comfortable."
Ha-Kim stared at her.
Ha-eun's mouth curved into the smallest smile.
He knew.
She knew he knew.
Neither needed to say it.
Ha-Kim pulled the blanket slightly higher around her.
"Good night."
"Good night."
A few minutes later, her breathing became slow and steady.
Ha-Kim remained awake a little longer.
He looked toward the rain-covered window.
For a brief moment, the words from his vision returned.
Found you.
His expression tightened.
Then Ha-eun moved closer in her sleep.
Ha-Kim looked down at her.
The thought faded.
He closed his eyes.
Neither of them knew that somewhere else in Seoul, someone was still searching.
And this time—
they were getting closer.
