# A New Beginning

A little girl was running through deep, dark alleys.
She was crying in fear, as if someone was hunting her.
“CATCH HER!” a loud voice shouted from the start of the alley.
The little girl looked behind her and saw a man running toward her.
“No, they saw me!” the little girl cried.
She continued running with her little legs. She looked like she had barely reached the age of ten.
She was holding a piece of bread tightly in her hands.
She finally reached the end of the alley.
“Here she is!” the man shouted.
The little girl's eyes widened as the man raised his whip.
Until suddenly...
“WOOF! WOOF!”
Dogs were barking.
“HA-EUN!”
A girl's voice shouted from behind.
A girl who looked around fifteen years old stood there, holding the dogs' leashes. She rushed toward the man, ready to stop him.
She let go of the leashes.
The dogs ran toward the man at full speed, forcing him away from Ha-eun.
Little Ha-eun froze.
She was terrified by what was happening in front of her.
“SA-RAM, WATCH OUT!” Ha-eun screamed.
A police officer suddenly appeared behind Sa-ram.
Sa-ram panicked and started running.
A gunshot rang through the alley.
Sa-ram fell.
Her death happened right in front of little Ha-eun.
That moment became stuck in Ha-eun's mind forever.
Ha-eun ran.
She never looked back again.

---

A girl suddenly woke up on a cracked, uncomfortable bed in a small apartment in Seoul.
It was Ha-eun.
She had grown up.
“Ughhh... this bed is so uncomfortable. I wish I could live in those fancy buildings I see on the internet.”
She went to the bathroom, brushed her teeth, and took a warm shower.
“I really need to find a new job.”
She picked up her phone and started searching for work.
Then she found an advertisement.
HIRING NOW
New Internship Opportunity
Pass the test and receive training at one of Seoul's biggest cybersecurity companies with the opportunity to receive a high-paying full-time position.
Ha-eun stared at the screen.
“I don't think I'll ever be accepted into something like that. I barely know how to write or talk properly.”
She sighed.
“I need to get ready for work.”
She got dressed and headed to her part-time job at a construction site.
Ha-eun was strong and determined. Nothing had stopped her before.
She left her apartment, walked to the bus stop, and took the bus to the construction site.
“Good afternoon, Mrs. Choi,” one of the workers greeted her.
“Hi, boss. Welcome,” another worker said.
“Hi, guys,” Ha-eun replied.
Her boss walked toward her.
“Choi Ha-eun, come here,” he said.
“What is wrong, sir?” she asked.
“You're fired.”
“What do you mean I'm fired?! Why?”
“Due to the company's new policies and guidelines, women are no longer allowed to work at construction sites. We're worried about their safety.”
Ha-eun stared at him for a moment.
Then she nodded.
She was broken.
Now she didn't have a job.
And she didn't know what the future was hiding for her.
She packed her things and went back home, hoping she could find another job.
She kept looking.
And looking.
For days, she searched for something suitable, but she couldn't find anything.
She hadn't even gone to high school.
She was completely alone.
Ha-eun sat in her apartment, staring hopelessly at her phone, wishing for a miracle.
Then the same advertisement appeared again.
This time, she clicked it.
It said:
APPLY NOW
No experience required. Get a chance to train at Haetae Cyber and earn a full-time position.
Ha-eun stared at the screen.
“I think I'll give it a shot.”
She clicked Apply.
A few seconds later, a message appeared.
Congratulations! You have successfully applied to Haetae Cyber.
Interview: Next Tuesday, 8:00 AM.
Ha-eun looked at her phone screen.
She smiled and nodded.
“I hope I get accepted.”

---

A few days later, it was finally interview day.
Ha-eun woke up super early and got ready for the interview.
She put on some makeup and styled her short black hair with subtle cyan streaks.
She looked stunning.
“I think I'm ready for this.”
She sounded confident, but deep inside, she was nervous.
“Time to leave and take the bus.”
Ha-eun left her apartment.
She looked confident on the outside, but inside, she was nervous and scared that she wouldn't get accepted.
She had almost no food left.
This was her last hope.
She took the bus, mentally preparing herself for the interview.
After some time, she arrived at a tall, enormous skyscraper.
It was Haetae Cyber.
“Wow... it's so big. Just like the ones I see on the internet.”
She looked up at the building.
“Will I really have a chance to work there...?”
At the entrance, a security guard noticed her.
“Hey! What are you doing here?” he shouted.
“I'm here for an interview,” Ha-eun replied.
The guard looked at her.
“Hmm. Go in.”
He watched her walk away.
No way she's here for an interview. She looks so out of place.
Ha-eun entered the building.
The reception area was enormous, almost twice the size of the entire building she lived in.
She looked around in amazement.
Then she heard a voice.
“Hey, you. Who are you?”
A tall, gloomy-looking man was standing nearby.
“I'm Choi Ha-eun. I'm here for an interview.”
The man looked at her.
Dear heavens... she looks miserable.
“Come with me,” he said.
Ha-eun followed him into a room filled with people waiting for their interviews.
“Here. Take your number.”
He handed her a piece of paper.
47
“Oh my God... 47.”
“That's your number in line. Wait until your turn comes.”
He left.
“Okay...”
Ha-eun sat down and waited.
Three hours and fifty minutes later...
“Number 47, please come in.”
Ha-eun stood up.
She opened the door and walked inside.
The room was empty.
There was only one desk, one chair, and a large screen in front of her.
The moment she entered, she felt something strange.
“Hello there. I'm the CEO of Haetae Cyber.”
A strange voice came from the screen.
“Hi...”
What the WTF? Was that a talking screen?
“What is your goal and purpose in working at our company?”
The voice came from the screen again.
Like we practiced, she told herself.
“I'm here because I'm desperate for money. I lost my job, I don't have any food left, and I really need this job.”
“Wha...”
Silence filled the room.
Suddenly...
“HAHAHAHAHAHA!”
A loud laugh came from the screen.
Ha-eun stared at it.
“Are you laughing...?”
She stood up.
She was angry.
There was so much frustration inside her, but she stayed silent.
She turned around and started walking toward the door.
“STOP!”
Ha-eun stopped and turned her head.
“You are hired. You're going to start tomorrow morning. Your training begins then.”
“What...?”
“Anyway, go home. Don't be late tomorrow. You'll receive a message on your phone with information about your trainer and everything you need to know before you start.”
Ha-eun stared at the screen in confusion.
What the hell? Is that motherfucker crazy? He was laughing at me because I'm poor, and now he hired me? What the hell? Is he a psychopath?
I think this was a bad idea.
She turned away and left the room.
Ha-eun went back to her apartment, still wondering what kind of person she had just been hired by.
