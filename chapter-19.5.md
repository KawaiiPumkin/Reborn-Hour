# Chapter 19.5 — Their First Date

Seo-jun had faced difficult clients.
He had survived university group projects.
He had once redesigned an entire website overnight because a client changed their mind at the last second.
None of those things prepared him for standing in front of his closet for twenty minutes.
He held up a black shirt.
"No."
He threw it onto the bed.
A white shirt.
"No."
Another shirt.
He stared at it.
"...Maybe."
He put it on.
Looked in the mirror.
Took it off.
"No."
He dropped onto the edge of his bed.
"What the hell do people wear on dates?"
His phone buzzed.
He picked it up.
A message from Ha-Kim.
Ha-Kim: You're late.
Seo-jun frowned.
Seo-jun: Late for what?
Ha-Kim: You said you'd come over.
Seo-jun looked at the time.
He had completely forgotten.
He sighed.
Seo-jun: I'm getting ready.
A response came almost immediately.
Ha-Kim: For the date?
Seo-jun stared at the screen.
Seo-jun: Obviously.
Ha-Kim: Then why are you texting me?
Seo-jun: Because I don't know what to wear.
There was a pause.
Then:
Ha-Kim: Wear clothes.
Seo-jun's eye twitched.
Seo-jun: You're useless.
Ha-Kim: You asked.
Seo-jun threw his phone onto the bed.
"Unbelievable."
───
About ten minutes later, Seo-jun stood outside Ha-Kim's apartment.
Ha-Kim opened the door.
He looked at Seo-jun.
Then at the shirt.
Then back at Seo-jun.
"What?"
Ha-Kim shook his head.
"Nothing."
Seo-jun walked inside.
"Don't lie."
"You look fine."
"Fine?"
"Yes."
"I'm going on a date."
"I know."
"With someone I actually like."
"I know."
"Shouldn't I look better than fine?"
Ha-Kim considered that.
"You look better than usual."
Seo-jun stared at him.
"Was that a compliment?"
"Yes."
"Wow."
Ha-eun was sitting on the couch.
She looked up.
"Oh."
Seo-jun pointed at Ha-Kim.
"Your boyfriend is useless."
Ha-eun blinked.
"Why?"
"He told me to wear clothes."
She looked at Seo-jun.
"That's actually good advice."
Seo-jun stared at her.
"You're supposed to help me."
Ha-eun smiled.
"Okay. Come here."
───
Ten minutes later, Seo-jun stood in front of the mirror again.
Ha-eun had changed his shirt, fixed his hair, and told him to stop trying so hard.
"You look good."
Seo-jun looked at himself.
"Really?"
"Really."
He smiled.
"Thank you."
Ha-eun nodded.
"Just don't talk too much."
Seo-jun looked offended.
"That's impossible."
Ha-Kim sat at the table.
"She's right."
Seo-jun pointed at him.
"You're both against me."
Ha-eun laughed softly.
"You'll be fine."
Seo-jun took a breath.
Then checked his phone.
"She's probably done with work soon."
Ha-Kim looked at him.
"You're nervous."
"No."
"You are."
Seo-jun sighed.
"Maybe."
Ha-Kim said nothing.
Seo-jun looked at him.
"What if it's awkward?"
"Then it'll be awkward."
"Great advice."
"What else do you want me to say?"
Seo-jun thought about it.
"Tell me I'm charming."
Ha-Kim looked at him.
"You're leaving."
Seo-jun laughed.
"Fair enough."
He turned toward the door.
Ha-eun called after him.
"Seo-jun."
He looked back.
"Yeah?"
"Have fun."
His expression softened.
"Thanks."
Then he left.
───
Across the city, Eun-bi had a similar problem.
She stood in front of her closet.
Three outfits were spread across her bed.
She picked up one.
Looked at it.
Put it down.
Picked up another.
She held it against herself.
Then looked at the mirror.
"No."
She sighed.
Her phone buzzed.
A message from her coworker.
Are you ready?
Eun-bi typed:
Almost.
The reply came immediately.
You've been saying that for thirty minutes.
Eun-bi stared at the message.
Then typed:
I don't know what to wear.
The response:
It's dinner, not a wedding.
Eun-bi smiled despite herself.
She looked back at the clothes.
Then picked a simple outfit.
Nothing too formal.
Nothing too casual.
Just something that felt like her.
She fixed her hair.
Looked in the mirror.
Her face felt warm.
"I'm really going on a date."
She covered her face with both hands.
"Why am I nervous?"
She already knew the answer.
Because it was him.
───
Seo-jun arrived outside the café early.
Very early.
He checked his phone.
6:41 PM.
Their plan was to meet at 7.
He sighed.
"Why did I come this early?"
He walked around for a few minutes.
Then came back.
6:47.
He checked his reflection in the café window.
Fixed his hair.
Then immediately stopped himself.
"Stop."
He put his hands into his pockets.
6:52.
The café door opened.
Seo-jun looked up.
Eun-bi stepped outside.
She looked around.
Then saw him.
She smiled.
Seo-jun forgot what he was about to say.
For a second, he just stood there.
Eun-bi walked toward him.
"Hi."
"Hi."
A small silence followed.
"You look nice."
Eun-bi looked down.
"Thank you."
"You too."
He smiled.
"You're... uh..."
He stopped himself.
Eun-bi looked up.
"What?"
"Nothing."
She smiled slightly.
"Okay."
Seo-jun laughed.
"Should we go?"
"Yeah."
They started walking.
Neither of them mentioned how nervous they were.
They didn't need to.
───
They chose a small restaurant a few streets away.
Nothing expensive.
Warm lights.
Quiet music.
Small tables close together.
Seo-jun pulled out a chair for Eun-bi.
She looked surprised.
"Thank you."
He sat across from her.
"You're welcome."
They looked at the menus.
For about thirty seconds, neither spoke.
Then Seo-jun looked up.
"Are you nervous?"
Eun-bi looked at him.
"A little."
"Me too."
She smiled.
"Really?"
"Very."
She laughed quietly.
"I didn't think you got nervous."
"I don't."
He paused.
"Usually."
She smiled again.
The tension slowly disappeared.
They started talking.
About work.
About his studio.
About the café.
About the customers she sometimes dealt with.
About the strangest things they'd seen during the week.
Seo-jun told her about a client who had changed the same logo seven times.
Eun-bi laughed.
"Seven?"
"Seven."
"What did you do?"
"I changed it back to the first version."
She covered her mouth.
"Did they notice?"
"After two days."
She laughed again.
Seo-jun smiled.
He liked that sound.
He hadn't realized how much.
───
After dinner, they walked through the city.
The streets were busy, but neither of them hurried.
Seo-jun bought them something sweet from a small shop.
Eun-bi took one bite.
"That's good."
"I told you."
"You were right."
He smiled proudly.
"I don't get that often."
"You probably should."
"See? This is why I like you."
Eun-bi stopped walking.
Seo-jun stopped too.
He realized what he'd said.
"...I mean."
She looked at him.
His face slowly turned red.
"I didn't mean—"
Eun-bi smiled.
"I know."
Seo-jun laughed nervously.
"That sounded better in my head."
She looked down.
"I liked it."
He became quiet.
They continued walking.
This time, their hands occasionally brushed against each other.
Neither moved away.
Eventually, Seo-jun slowly moved his hand closer.
Eun-bi noticed.
She hesitated.
Then gently held his hand.
Seo-jun looked at her.
She looked away.
Neither said anything.
They just kept walking.
───
They eventually reached a quieter street.
The city lights reflected against the windows of the buildings around them.
Seo-jun slowed down.
"Eun-bi."
She looked at him.
"Yeah?"
"Can I tell you something?"
She nodded.
"I didn't ask you out because I wanted to impress you."
She waited.
"I just wanted to spend time with you."
Her expression softened.
"I know."
Seo-jun smiled.
"Do you?"
She nodded.
"I was actually surprised you asked."
"Why?"
Eun-bi looked down.
"Because I thought you just liked coming to the café."
Seo-jun laughed softly.
"I did."
She looked up.
"The coffee was good."
He smiled.
"But..."
He squeezed her hand gently.
"I think I started going because you were there."
Eun-bi's eyes widened slightly.
Seo-jun continued.
"I didn't realize it at first."
He looked down.
"I just kept wanting to see you."
Eun-bi was quiet.
Then she smiled.
"I liked you before you asked."
Seo-jun froze.
"What?"
She immediately looked away.
"I said I liked you."
"For how long?"
Eun-bi became even more embarrassed.
"I don't know."
"A while?"
She nodded.
"Yeah."
Seo-jun laughed softly.
"You're kidding."
"I'm not."
"I thought you were just being nice."
"I was."
She looked at him.
"But I liked you too."
He smiled.
For once, Seo-jun didn't have a joke ready.
He just looked at her.
"I'm glad."
Eun-bi smiled.
"Me too."
───
They stood there for a moment.
Neither wanted to leave yet.
Seo-jun looked at her.
Then hesitated.
"Eun-bi."
"Yes?"
"Can I..."
He stopped.
She understood.
Her cheeks turned pink.
She nodded.
Seo-jun leaned closer.
Their first kiss was short and gentle.
When they pulled apart, Eun-bi immediately looked down.
Her face was bright red.
Seo-jun smiled.
"You're cute."
"Don't say that."
"Why?"
"Because I'm already embarrassed."
He laughed quietly.
"Sorry."
She looked at him.
Then, after a moment, she stepped closer.
Seo-jun blinked.
Before he could say anything, Eun-bi kissed him again.
This one lasted a little longer.
When they separated, Seo-jun looked completely stunned.
Eun-bi immediately looked away.
"I..."
He smiled.
"That was unexpected."
She covered part of her face.
"Forget it."
"No."
He gently took her hand again.
"I'm definitely not forgetting that."
She smiled behind her hand.
───
Eventually, Seo-jun walked her home.
They stopped outside her building.
"I had fun."
"Me too."
Eun-bi hesitated.
Then looked at him.
"Can we do this again?"
Seo-jun smiled.
"Definitely."
She nodded.
"Good."
She started walking toward the entrance.
Then turned around.
"Good night."
"Good night."
She smiled and went inside.
Seo-jun stayed there for a few seconds.
Then pulled out his phone.
He opened Ha-Kim's chat.
Seo-jun: I think I have a girlfriend.
The response came quickly.
Ha-Kim: You don't.
Seo-jun frowned.
Seo-jun: What do you mean?
Ha-Kim: You haven't asked her to be your girlfriend.
Seo-jun stared at the message.
Seo-jun: Bro.
Ha-Kim: What?
Seo-jun: Let me have this.
A pause.
Then:
Ha-Kim: Fine.
Seo-jun smiled.
Another message appeared.
Ha-Kim: But technically, you're still wrong.
Seo-jun laughed.
Seo-jun: Good night, Ha-Kim.
Ha-Kim: Good night.
Seo-jun put his phone away.
He looked up at the apartment building.
He smiled.
For the first time, the feeling wasn't confusing anymore.
He liked Eun-bi.
She liked him.
And whatever happened next...
He wanted to find out.
