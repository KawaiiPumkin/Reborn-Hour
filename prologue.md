# The Angel Was Born

It was a rainy night in the middle of a typical Seoul winter.
Ha Kim was born.

Nurse: “Congratulations, ma’am. Your newborn is here.”
Baby Ha Kim was born, shining as if he were a little star sent down to Earth.

San Hyun: “My baby…”
As she held her newborn, she stared at him in awe.
He looked almost majestic. Everyone in the operating room was amazed by the way he looked as if he didn't belong to this world.
Yet, baby Ha-Kim had no idea what this world was keeping from him.
