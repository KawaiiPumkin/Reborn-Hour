Chapter 30 — The Future I Choose
The man raised the blade.
Ha-Kim stepped forward.
"Get away from her."
The man stared at him.
Then laughed.
"You?"
Ha-Kim didn't answer.
Ha-eun's eyes stayed fixed on him.
"Ha-Kim..."
Her voice was barely audible.
He glanced at her.
"Are you hurt?"
She shook her head quickly.
"I'm okay."
He looked back at the man.
"Stay behind me."
"But—"
"Ha-eun."
His voice was firm.
She stopped.
The man suddenly lunged.
Ha-Kim moved.
The blade came toward him.
He dodged sideways and grabbed the man's wrist.
The two struggled.
The attacker was stronger than Ha-Kim expected.
He twisted free and swung again.
Ha-Kim stepped back.
The blade missed his chest by inches.
Ha-eun screamed.
"Ha-Kim!"
"I'm fine."
He grabbed the man's arm again.
This time, he twisted it hard.
The blade fell.
It hit the floor with a metallic sound.
The second man rushed forward.
Ha-Kim saw him coming.
He turned.
A punch landed against his jaw.
His head snapped sideways.
He stumbled.
Ha-eun gasped.
"Ha-Kim!"
He wiped the blood from his mouth.
Then looked at the man.
"That all?"
The man charged again.
Ha-Kim stepped forward instead.
His fist connected with the man's stomach.
The man doubled over.
Ha-Kim grabbed the back of his jacket and threw him against the wall.
The room went silent.
For a second.
Then—
The first attacker picked up the blade.
Ha-Kim turned.
Too late.
The man rushed toward Ha-eun.
Ha-Kim moved without thinking.
He stepped between them.
The blade went into him.
Everything stopped.
Ha-eun's eyes widened.
"Ha-Kim..."
He looked down.
The blade was buried in his side.
His breath caught.
The attacker pulled it out.
Ha-Kim staggered.
Ha-eun screamed.
"HA-KIM!"
He fell to one knee.
Blood spread across his clothes.
Ha-eun ran toward him.
"Don't!"
Ha-Kim caught her wrist.
"Stay back."
"But you're bleeding!"
"I'm fine."
"You are not fine!"
His grip weakened.
The attacker raised the blade again.
Ha-Kim looked up.
His expression changed.
He forced himself to stand.
"You don't get another chance."
He grabbed the man's wrist.
The attacker struggled.
Ha-Kim twisted.
The blade dropped.
Then Ha-Kim struck him.
Once.
Twice.
The man fell.
Ha-Kim stood over him, breathing heavily.
The second attacker tried to get up.
Ha-Kim turned.
"Don't."
The man froze.
Ha-Kim's voice was quiet.
"Stay down."
The man stayed down.
Ha-Kim's legs suddenly weakened.
He grabbed the wall.
Ha-eun rushed to him.
"Ha-Kim!"
She caught him before he fell.
"You're bleeding so much."
"I'm okay."
"Stop saying that!"
Her voice broke.
He looked at her.
She was crying.
"I'm sorry."
Ha-eun shook her head.
"Don't apologize."
She pressed both hands against the wound.
"Just stay awake."
Ha-Kim looked down at her hands.
Then at her face.
"You're safe."
"I don't care about that right now!"
She looked terrified.
"You're the one bleeding."
Ha-Kim tried to smile.
"I'm still here."
Sirens sounded in the distance.
Ha-eun looked toward the door.
"I called the police."
Ha-Kim nodded.
"Good."
The sound grew louder.
Then the door burst open.
Police officers rushed inside.
"Drop everything!"
The remaining attackers were immediately restrained.
One officer ran toward Ha-Kim and Ha-eun.
"Sir, you've been stabbed."
Ha-eun looked at him.
"He needs an ambulance."
"We're getting one."
Ha-Kim's vision blurred.
The room began spinning.
He heard voices around him.
Someone was asking questions.
Someone was calling for medical equipment.
Ha-eun kept holding his hand.
"Ha-Kim."
He looked at her.
"I'm here."
Her eyes were full of tears.
"Don't close your eyes."
He squeezed her hand.
"I'm not going anywhere."
But his vision continued fading.
The sound of the rain became distant.
The lights above him blurred.
Then—
Pain.
Not from the wound.
Something deeper.
His head suddenly felt like it was splitting apart.
Ha-Kim gasped.
He couldn't breathe.
His vision flashed.
The future.
Again.
Hundreds of images.
Thousands.
Too many.
His power was reacting.
He had changed it.
He had changed the future.
And the cost had finally arrived.
His body shook.
"Ha-Kim!"
Ha-eun held him tighter.
"What's happening?"
He couldn't answer.
His eyes lost focus.
For the first time since his ability awakened—
he couldn't see the future.
There was only darkness.
───
The ambulance arrived.
Ha-eun climbed inside with him.
She held his hand the entire way.
"Stay with me."
Ha-Kim barely heard her.
His breathing became weaker.
The paramedics worked around him.
"Blood pressure dropping."
"Keep him awake."
"He's losing too much blood."
Ha-eun's tears fell onto his hand.
"Please."
Ha-Kim slowly opened his eyes.
He looked at her.
She was the only thing he could see clearly.
"Ha-eun."
"I'm here."
He tried to speak.
Nothing came out.
She leaned closer.
"What?"
His lips barely moved.
"I... made it."
Ha-eun shook her head.
"Don't talk."
He closed his eyes.
She squeezed his hand.
"Please."
───
The hospital doors opened.
Doctors rushed him inside.
Ha-eun was left standing outside.
Her clothes were soaked.
Her hands were covered in his blood.
She stared at them.
Then at the doors.
For the first time in her life—
she had no idea what to do.
She slowly sat down.
Her hands trembled.
"Please..."
Inside the operating room, Ha-Kim fought to stay alive.
But something else was happening.
Something much larger.
His body was paying the price.
Not for seeing the future.
For changing it.
And this time—
the price would not be a headache.
It would not be a fever.
It would not be his eyesight.
It would be years.
Years of his life.
His heart slowed.
The monitors screamed.
Doctors moved faster.
"He's crashing!"
───
Outside, Ha-eun suddenly stood.
"Ha-Kim!"
She stared at the operating room doors.
Nothing.
No answer.
Only the sound of rain against the hospital windows.
And behind those doors—
the man she loved was fighting for a future he had chosen.
Not one he had seen.
Not one he had calculated.
His own.
