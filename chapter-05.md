# Chapter 5 — Don't Give Up

The next morning, Ha-eun arrived earlier than usual.
She sat at her desk and looked at the computer.
Everything seemed normal.
Too normal.
She opened the training files Ha-Kim had given her.
Yesterday had gone well.
She had found a mistake in a security report.
Ha-Kim had actually told her she was right.
For some reason, she had been thinking about that all morning.
She smiled slightly.
“Maybe I'm getting better.”
She opened the first file.
Then the second.
Then the third.
The instructions looked simple.
At least, they looked simple when Ha-Kim explained them.
Ha-eun started working.
Five minutes passed.
Then ten.
She clicked through the system carefully.
She remembered what Ha-Kim had taught her.
Check the permissions.
Check the access logs.
Compare the information.
Don't assume something is wrong just because it looks strange.
She nodded to herself.
“I can do this.”
She continued.

───

Around thirty minutes later, Ha-Kim walked toward her desk.
He looked at her screen.
“You started already?”
Ha-eun nodded.
“Yeah.”
“Good.”
She smiled.
“I think I understand it now.”
Ha-Kim looked at the screen again.
“Show me.”
She explained what she had done.
He listened without interrupting.
Then he pointed at one part.
“Why did you do this?”
Ha-eun looked at it.
“Because the access level looked wrong.”
“Did you check it?”
She paused.
“No.”
“So you guessed.”
Ha-eun's smile disappeared.
“...Yeah.”
“Don't guess.”
“Right.”
She quickly changed it.
Ha-Kim nodded.
“Continue.”
He walked away.
Ha-eun watched him leave.
“That's it?”
He turned around.
“What?”
“You're not going to tell me if I'm doing it right?”
“You'll know when you're finished.”
She stared at him.
“You're annoying.”
“I know.”
He returned to his desk.
Ha-eun sighed.
Then continued working.

───

An hour later, she finally finished.
She looked over everything twice.
Then clicked submit.
A small message appeared.
ERROR.
Ha-eun froze.
“No.”
She checked the file.
Then the system.
Another error appeared.
Her eyes widened.
“What did I do?”
She started checking everything.
Nothing made sense.
She called Ha-Kim.
“Ha-Kim.”
He looked over.
“What?”
“I think something went wrong.”
He stood up and walked over.
He looked at the screen.
His expression changed slightly.
Not anger.
Just concern.
“What happened?”
“I don't know.”
He looked through the report.
Then at the system.
“You changed the access permissions.”
Ha-eun nodded.
“Yeah.”
“Why?”
“Because I thought they were wrong.”
“They weren't.”
Her face went pale.
“Oh.”
Ha-Kim continued looking.
She waited.
He finally pointed at the screen.
“You changed this.”
“I know.”
“This affected the rest of the system.”
Ha-eun swallowed.
“How bad?”
“Bad enough.”
Her stomach dropped.
She looked at the screen again.
“I messed everything up.”
Ha-Kim didn't answer immediately.
Ha-eun stood up.
“I'll fix it.”
She started clicking through the menus.
“Stop.”
She froze.
“Don't touch anything.”
“Okay.”
Ha-Kim took a few steps back.
“Look at what you changed.”
She stared at the screen.
“I don't know where to start.”
“Start with the first thing you changed.”
She looked through the settings.
Nothing.
“I can't find it.”
“Look again.”
She searched.
Still nothing.
“I can't.”
Ha-Kim stayed quiet.
Ha-eun looked at him.
“Can you just fix it?”
“No.”
She blinked.
“What?”
“You caused the problem.”
“I know.”
“So fix it.”
Her eyes widened.
“But I don't know how.”
“You do.”
“No, I don't.”
“You just forgot.”
She looked back at the screen.
Her hands started shaking slightly.
She couldn't understand what she had done.
Everyone around them was working.
Nobody was paying attention.
But Ha-eun could already imagine what they would think if they found out.
She had no qualifications.
No proper education.
Barely any experience.
And now she had managed to cause a serious problem at one of the biggest cybersecurity companies in Seoul.
She looked at Ha-Kim.
“I shouldn't be here.”
He didn't respond.
Ha-eun lowered her head.
“I knew this would happen.”
“Ha-eun.”
“I don't know what I'm doing.”
“You're learning.”
“I'm not.”
“You are.”
“No.”
Her voice became quieter.
“I keep making mistakes.”
Ha-Kim looked at her.
“So?”
She looked up.
“What do you mean, so?”
“You made a mistake.”
“A serious one.”
“Yes.”
Her eyes dropped again.
“I can't do this.”
Ha-Kim was quiet for a moment.
Then he pulled a chair beside her.
“Sit.”
She sat down.
He pointed at the screen.
“Tell me what you changed.”
Ha-eun looked at the settings.
“I changed the access level.”
“Why?”
“I thought it was wrong.”
“Was it?”
“No.”
“Why did you think it was wrong?”
She looked carefully.
“Because... the number was different from the others.”
“Good.”
She looked at him.
“Good?”
“You noticed something unusual.”
“But I was wrong.”
“You were wrong about what it meant.”
Ha-eun stared at the screen.
Ha-Kim continued.
“There's a difference.”
She didn't answer.
“Now check the original report.”
She opened it.
“Compare them.”
She did.
“Now find the first thing that changed.”
Ha-eun slowly moved through the information.
“Here.”
Ha-Kim nodded.
“Good.”
She continued.
“Then this one changed because of it.”
“Yes.”
“And this one changed after that.”
“Yes.”
She looked at him.
“So if I fix the first one...”
“The rest should return.”
She hesitated.
“Should?”
“Check it.”
Ha-eun looked back at the screen.
She carefully changed the setting.
Then waited.
The system refreshed.
One error disappeared.
Then another.
Ha-eun's eyes widened.
“It worked.”
Ha-Kim nodded.
She checked the report again.
Everything was back to normal.
Ha-eun leaned back in her chair.
She let out a long breath.
“I fixed it.”
“You did.”
She looked at him.
“You didn't even touch it.”
“No.”
“You could've fixed it in like thirty seconds.”
“Yes.”
“Then why didn't you?”
Ha-Kim looked at her.
“Because then you wouldn't learn anything.”
Ha-eun went quiet.
She looked at the screen.
Then at him.
“I thought you were going to be angry.”
“I was.”
Her eyes widened.
“Oh.”
“But you're training to work here.”
He pointed at the screen.
“You're going to make mistakes.”
Ha-eun looked down.
“Even serious ones?”
“Especially serious ones.”
She didn't understand.
Ha-Kim leaned back slightly.
“If you never make mistakes, you're not learning anything new.”
Ha-eun looked at him.
“That's what you think?”
“It's obvious.”
She gave him a tired look.
“Everything is obvious to you.”
“Not everything.”
She almost smiled.
Then looked at the screen again.
“Still...”
“What?”
“I thought you were going to tell the manager.”
Ha-Kim looked at her.
“Why?”
“Because I messed up.”
“You fixed it.”
“Because you helped.”
“I didn't fix it.”
“But you told me what to do.”
“I asked questions.”
Ha-eun looked at him.
“That still helped.”
“Maybe.”
She smiled slightly.
“Thank you.”
Ha-Kim nodded.
“Finish the report.”
“Right.”
He stood up.
Then stopped.
“Ha-eun.”
She looked at him.
“Don't give up just because something is difficult.”
She stared at him.
His expression was the same as always.
Calm.
Unreadable.
Like he had just said something completely normal.
Then he walked back to his desk.
Ha-eun watched him.
She looked down at the report.
Then back at him.
She quietly smiled.

───

The rest of the afternoon went better.
Ha-eun checked everything twice.
Then a third time.
She asked fewer questions.
When something looked wrong, she didn't immediately assume it was broken.
She checked.
Compared.
Thought.
Then acted.
Ha-Kim noticed.
He didn't say anything.
He didn't need to.
Near the end of the day, Ha-eun finished another task.
She looked at the result.
No errors.
She looked at Ha-Kim.
“Finished.”
He checked it.
“Good.”
That word again.
Good.
For some reason, it felt different this time.
Ha-eun smiled.
“I actually did it.”
“You did.”
She looked down at her hands.
She was still inexperienced.
Still slow.
Still made mistakes.
She still didn't know half of what everyone else seemed to know.
But she had fixed something.
Not Ha-Kim.
Not someone else.
Her.
She had fixed it.
Ha-eun looked toward Ha-Kim's desk.
He was already working again.
She didn't know why he believed she could do it.
Maybe he didn't think she was talented.
Maybe he just thought she could learn.
Either way...
Someone believed she could improve.
And for the first time in a long time, Ha-eun started believing it too.

───

Ha-Kim looked at his screen.
Then briefly glanced toward Ha-eun.
She was smiling at her computer.
He turned back.
“She'll be fine.”
Then he continued working.
