# Chapter 15 — Min-seo

For a few seconds, neither of them spoke.

Ha-Kim stood still.

Min-seo looked at him carefully.

She had expected him to change.
People always did.

But this was different.

The man standing in front of her didn't look like the Ha-Kim she remembered.

His hair was longer.
He wore glasses now.
His clothes were darker.
His expression was harder to read.

Even the way he stood felt different.
More controlled.
More distant.

Min-seo slowly smiled.

"You really changed."

Ha-Kim said nothing.

She took another step closer.

"I almost didn't recognize you."

"Then you shouldn't have stopped me."

Her smile faded slightly.

There it was.
That coldness.

But even that wasn't quite the same.

The old Ha-Kim could be quiet.
He could be distant.

But he wasn't this closed off.

Min-seo looked into his eyes.

"Are you really going to pretend you don't know me?"

"I know you."

"Then say my name."

Ha-Kim stared at her.

"Min-seo."

Hearing it from him made her expression soften.

She had missed that voice.
More than she wanted to admit.

"You're still the same."

"No."

She looked at him.

"No?"

"I'm not."

Min-seo smiled faintly.

"You really aren't."

A car passed behind them.
People continued walking around them, completely unaware of what was happening.

Min-seo glanced toward a nearby café.

"Can we talk?"

"I don't think that's a good idea."

"Why?"

"Because there's nothing to talk about."

She looked at him for a long moment.

"That's not true."

Ha-Kim didn't answer.

Min-seo crossed her arms.

"You disappeared."

"I didn't disappear."

"You stopped answering."

"You stopped asking."

Her expression changed.

That one hurt.

She looked away.

"You're still good at that."

"At what?"

"Making one sentence sound like an entire argument."

Ha-Kim looked away.

"I have somewhere to be."

"With someone?"

He looked at her.

Min-seo noticed the slight pause.

Her eyes narrowed.

"There's someone."

"No."

"You hesitated."

"I didn't."

"You did."

Ha-Kim sighed.

"I'm going home."

Min-seo smiled slightly.

"Can I walk with you?"

"No."

She followed him anyway.

Ha-Kim noticed after a few steps.

"You're coming."

"I know."

"I said no."

"I heard you."

He stopped walking.

She stopped too.

For a moment, they simply stared at each other.

Then Min-seo smiled.

"You really did change."

Ha-Kim continued walking.

She followed.

───

The walk was quiet.

Min-seo stayed beside him.

She looked at him from time to time.

Ha-Kim noticed every time.

"What?"

"Nothing."

"You keep looking at me."

"I'm trying to figure you out."

"You won't."

She smiled.

"You used to let me."

Ha-Kim didn't respond.

That sentence stayed between them.

Used to.

Min-seo looked down.

"How long has it been?"

"Almost two years."

She nodded.

"Feels longer."

"Probably."

She glanced at him.

"Are you happy?"

He stopped.

The question caught him off guard.

Min-seo looked at him seriously.

"Are you?"

Ha-Kim didn't answer immediately.

His mind went somewhere else.

A small apartment.
A girl sitting at his kitchen table.
Someone complaining about his cooking.
Someone telling him to take medicine.
Someone smiling because he said her food was good.
Someone who had started knocking on his door simply because she wanted to see him.

Ha-eun.

His expression softened without him noticing.

Min-seo saw it.

Her eyes lowered slightly.

"You are."

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"What?"

"You're happy."

He said nothing.

She smiled sadly.

"I don't think I've ever seen you look like that."

"Like what?"

"Like you actually want to be somewhere."

Ha-Kim looked away.

Min-seo continued walking.

"I remember you always wanted to leave."

"People change."

"I can see that."

She was quiet for a moment.

Then she asked,

"Do you hate me?"

Ha-Kim stopped again.

"No."

The answer came immediately.

Min-seo looked at him.

"No?"

"No."

"Then why did you leave?"

He looked down at the sidewalk.

"Because staying wasn't good for either of us."

She swallowed.

"I didn't understand that back then."

"I know."

"And now?"

"Now I do."

Min-seo looked at him.

There was something different in his eyes.

He wasn't angry.
He wasn't bitter.
He wasn't trying to punish her.

That somehow hurt more.

Because it meant he had already moved forward.

"Do you still care about me?"

Ha-Kim went silent.

The honest answer was complicated.

He didn't hate her.
He never had.

There were still memories.
Late nights.
Arguments.
Quiet moments.
Her hand in his.
The way she used to lean against him when she was tired.
The first time she smiled at him.
The first time he realized he was completely attached to her.

Those memories hadn't disappeared.

They had simply become old.

"I don't know."

Min-seo looked down.

"That's fair."

Ha-Kim started walking again.

She followed.

───

They reached the apartment building.

Min-seo looked up.

"You live here?"

"Yes."

"Alone?"

"Yes."

She smiled.

"Some things never change."

Ha-Kim entered the building.

Min-seo followed him to the elevator.

The doors opened.

They stepped inside.

Ha-Kim pressed his floor.

Min-seo watched him.

"You're really different."

"You've said that."

"Because I can't stop noticing it."

The elevator moved upward.

"You seem..."

She paused.

"Calmer."

Ha-Kim didn't answer.

"Happier."

Still nothing.

"And less lonely."

He looked at her.

Min-seo smiled faintly.

"That one bothered you."

"No."

"It did."

The elevator stopped.

The doors opened.

Ha-Kim stepped out.

Min-seo followed.

He stopped in front of his apartment.

She looked around.

"Nice place."

"It's fine."

"You always said you wanted somewhere quiet."

"I got it."

She smiled.

Then her expression became serious.

"Can I see you again?"

Ha-Kim looked at her.

"Why?"

"Because I want to."

He stared at her.

Min-seo took a breath.

"I know things between us weren't perfect."

"They weren't."

"I know I made mistakes."

Ha-Kim remained silent.

"I didn't understand you back then."

"You weren't the only one."

She looked surprised.

"You blame yourself too?"

"Sometimes."

"For what?"

He looked at the door.

"For staying when I should've left."

Min-seo's expression fell.

But Ha-Kim continued.

"And leaving when I should've talked."

She looked at him for a long time.

Then she smiled faintly.

"Maybe we both messed up."

"Maybe."

She stepped closer.

"Then let me fix my part."

Ha-Kim didn't move.

"I don't know if you can."

"I can try."

He looked at her.

For a moment, he saw the girl he used to know.

Not the woman standing in front of him.

The memory.
The warmth.
The comfort.
The pain.

Everything at once.

Part of him still cared.

That was the problem.

If he felt nothing, this would have been easy.

Min-seo took a small step back.

"I'll see you again."

Ha-Kim didn't answer.

She turned and walked toward the elevator.

Before the doors closed, she looked back at him.

"Goodnight, Ha-Kim."

"Goodnight."

The doors closed.

Ha-Kim stood alone in the hallway.

He looked at his apartment door.
Then at the elevator.

His hand slowly reached for the doorknob.

He stopped.

For some reason, he thought about Ha-eun.

Her voice.
Her smile.
The way she had looked at him earlier.
The way she had told him to eat.

He opened his door.

The apartment was quiet.

But for the first time, the quiet didn't feel empty.

He walked inside.

His phone vibrated.

A message.

Ha-eun: Did you eat?

Ha-Kim stared at the screen.

A small smile appeared.

Ha-Kim: Yes.

Three dots appeared.

Ha-eun: Good :)

He looked at the message for a few seconds.

Then another thought crossed his mind.

Min-seo was back.

And he still cared about her.

But somehow...

When Ha-eun asked if he had eaten, that was the message he wanted to see.
