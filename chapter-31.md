# Chapter 31 — Ten Years
The hospital hallway was quiet.
Ha-eun sat outside the operating room, staring at the floor.
Her hands were still stained with Ha-Kim's blood.
Seo-jun arrived first.
He was out of breath.
"Ha-eun."
She looked up.
He stopped when he saw her.
He didn't ask what happened.
He already knew something was wrong.
A few minutes later, Ji-woo arrived.
Then Ji-hoon.
Even Min-hyuk came.
One by one, people who cared about Ha-Kim gathered outside the same door.
Nobody knew what to say.
Finally, the operating room doors opened.
A doctor stepped outside.
Everyone stood.
"He survived the surgery."
Ha-eun's legs almost gave out.
"He's alive?"
The doctor nodded.
"Yes."
She covered her mouth.
Tears immediately filled her eyes.
"But..."
The word made everyone freeze.
The doctor looked at them carefully.
"The injuries were severe."
He paused.
"His body went through an extreme amount of stress."
Ha-eun's fingers tightened.
"Will he wake up?"
The doctor was silent for a moment.
"We don't know."
Her face went pale.
"He has fallen into a coma."
Silence.
Ha-eun stared at him.
"A coma..."
"We'll continue monitoring him."
She looked toward the room.
"Can I see him?"
The doctor nodded.
───
Ha-Kim lay motionless beneath the white hospital sheets.
Machines surrounded him.
His eyes were closed.
Ha-eun sat beside him and held his hand.
It was warm.
That was enough.
She lowered her head.
"You promised you'd come home."
No response.
She smiled through her tears.
"So..."
Her fingers tightened around his.
"I'm waiting."
Outside the room, Seo-jun stood silently.
He looked through the glass.
For once, he had nothing to say.
Years would pass.
Then more.
And Ha-eun would still be there.
Waiting.
───
One year.
Three years.
Five.
The world changed.
People grew older.
Companies grew.
Cities changed.
Technology advanced.
Seasons came and went.
But inside one hospital room—
Ha-Kim remained asleep.
Ten years would pass before he opened his eyes again.
